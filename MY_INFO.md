# TMMCH Chatbot Knowledge Base

This file is the chatbot's knowledge base about **Tairunnessa Memorial Medical College &
Hospital (TMMCH)** in Gazipur, Bangladesh. The bot reads this file on every message and
answers using it. Update this file to teach the bot new information — no restart needed.

## A. Basic Institutional Information

**Q1. What is TMMCH?**
Tairunnessa Memorial Medical College & Hospital (TMMCH) is a medical education and healthcare institution located in Gazipur, Bangladesh.

**Q2. Where is TMMCH located?**
TMMCH is located at Konia (Targach), Board Bazar, Gazipur-1704, Bangladesh.

**Q3. Where exactly is the campus situated?**
The campus is near Board Bazar on the Dhaka–Mymensingh Highway, just outside Dhaka.

**Q4. How far is TMMCH from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport?**
The college website states that the campus is approximately 9 km from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport.

**Q5. Is the hospital on the same campus?**
Yes. The medical college and teaching hospital are part of the same institutional campus.

**Q6. What is the main objective of TMMCH?**
The college aims to provide broad-based medical education and develop the knowledge, skills and attitudes needed for professional medical practice and future training.

## B. MBBS Admission

**Q7. Does TMMCH offer MBBS?**
Yes. TMMCH offers an MBBS course.

**Q8. How are local students admitted?**
Admission is based on merit and follows the criteria prescribed by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and Bangladesh Medical & Dental Council.

**Q9. What educational qualifications are required for local admission?**
Applicants need SSC/equivalent and HSC/equivalent science education with Physics, Chemistry and Biology, subject to the admission criteria announced by the relevant authorities.

**Q10. What subjects are included in the admission test?**
The college website states that the written admission test consists of MCQs from: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, General Knowledge.

**Q11. Is admission based only on the admission test?**
No. Final selection considers admission-test performance together with SSC and HSC examination results.

**Q12. Where can candidates obtain admission information?**
Admission information is available through the Principal's Office and the college's official admission pages.

**Q13. Does TMMCH accept online admission applications?**
Yes. The official website provides an MBBS Online Admission form.

**Q14. Does TMMCH admit foreign students?**
Yes. TMMCH has a separate foreign-student admission process.

**Q15. What is the duration of MBBS for foreign students?**
The current foreign-admission page describes the MBBS programme as 6 years: 5 years of study plus 1 year of compulsory internship training.

**Q16. What is the current foreign-student batch mentioned by TMMCH?**
The current foreign-student admission page identifies Session 2026–2027, Batch TM-25.

**Q17. What are the foreign-student admission fees?**
The current website lists the applicable fees for the 2026–2027 session. Because fees can change, always direct applicants to the latest official admission notice rather than permanently storing fee amounts.
